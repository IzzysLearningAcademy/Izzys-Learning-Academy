# Izzy's Learning Academy — V6.7 Development Plan

Status: Development branch opened. NOT deployed to main.
Baseline: V6.6 main. Preserve existing localStorage keys and progress.

## Release strategy
Early Access: progressive word search, expanded Language Lab, varied Math/ELA.
Full: STEM/Tech Discovery Lab and Creative Studio tools.

## Acceptance criteria
### Word Search
- At least 6 distinct levels per theme; word counts progress 6, 8, 10, 12, 15, 15–20.
- New words and regenerated grids; avoid repeating the same six words on replay.
- Completing every word displays a completion state and a functional Next Level action.
- Save level completion and resume state locally without overwriting existing progress.
- Touch-friendly on iPad Safari; validate grid placement and selections.

### Language Lab
- Expand beyond introductory vocabulary into themed units: numbers, greetings, colors, family, food, animals, school, questions.
- Teach vocabulary with meaning, pronunciation (where supported), review and quizzes.
- Track mastery and advance through actual authored content; avoid placeholder levels.
- ASL is visual, not spoken; label guidance accordingly.

### Math and ELA
- Introduce fresh question pools, progressive difficulty, review of missed skills.
- Preserve multiplication current/best streak fixes and original storage key.

### STEM/Tech
- Interactive Code Quest with sequences and loops.
- Engineering and science tasks with predictions, feedback and increasing challenge.
- Tech literacy and age-appropriate online safety/AI literacy.

### Creative Studio
- Expand palettes and adjustable brush widths in BOTH Calm Garden coloring and free painting.
- Undo, eraser and precision controls. Tap-to-fill enclosed regions in coloring pages.
- Preserve saved art and ensure finger/Apple Pencil input works.

## Release gates
- Test on iPhone and iPad Safari.
- Verify no regression to notebook, Calm Garden, star rewards, Language Lab, Math/ELA, and local progress.
- Review student privacy before expanding accounts or collecting child data.
- Deploy only after test sign-off; do not modify main during development.
